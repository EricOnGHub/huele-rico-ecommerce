Frequently Asked Questions

Are the fragrances authentic?

Yes—Huele Rico! is being built to sell genuine, original designer fragrances, not replicas, imitations or Huele Rico!-branded perfumes. The basis for this promise is a documented sourcing-and-verification process: purchasing from vetted suppliers with traceable invoices, checking the product and packaging against the manufacturer's identifying information, and handling customer concerns through a clear returns and authenticity-review process. These checks must be completed and documented for every live supplier and product before sales begin. A batch code by itself does not prove authenticity. Our current website displays an illustrative catalog; specific inventory has not yet been verified or offered for sale.

Why are the prices lower?

Our goal is to offer attractive prices through efficient sourcing and online operations. We will only advertise percentage savings against real, documented, comparable retail prices. Sample prices on this preview are not live offers.

Does Huele Rico! manufacture these fragrances?

No. Huele Rico! is an independent multi-brand retailer, not the manufacturer of the designer fragrances shown. Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How does shipping work?

We will publish eligible destinations, shipping rates, transit estimates and fragrance-safe shipping methods before accepting orders. Some fragrances require special ground-shipping handling.

Can I return a fragrance?

A complete returns and refund policy will be published before launch, including eligibility, time limits, damaged packages and authenticity disputes. See Shipping & Returns for the current status.

How can I get help?

Our Contact Us page includes a bilingual inquiry form. Message delivery will be activated once a verified support inbox is connected; this preview does not yet receive submissions.
