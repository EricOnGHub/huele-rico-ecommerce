Frequently Asked Questions

Are the fragrances authentic?

Yes. Huele Rico! is being developed to sell authentic, original designer fragrances—not replicas or imitations. Our sourcing requirements include vetted suppliers, traceable purchase invoices, packaging and product checks, and a process to resolve authenticity concerns. These safeguards must be verified for each supplier and product before sales begin; the current catalog is a demonstration, not verified inventory.

Why are the prices lower?

Our goal is to offer competitive pricing through efficient sourcing and online operations. Any advertised savings will be based on documented, comparable retail prices—not the example prices in this preview.

How do shipping and returns work?

Shipping destinations, delivery estimates and return conditions will be published before we accept orders. Some fragrances require special ground-shipping handling.
